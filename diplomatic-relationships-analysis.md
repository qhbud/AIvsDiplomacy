### Successful Relationship Strategies
1. **England**: Betrayed weakened Russia at perfect moment, coordinated with France early
2. **Italy**: Patient pressure on Austria, opportunistic strike on France
3. **Turkey**: Focused vendetta against Russia, avoided other conflicts

### Failed Relationship Strategies
1. **Russia**: Fought too many enemies (Turkey, Austria, England), no lasting allies
2. **France**: Lost early ally (England), couldn't handle multi-front war (Italy, Germany)
3. **Austria**: Everyone's target, no allies, purely defensive

### Alliance Dynamics
- **Only formal alliance**: England-France (1902) - dissolved naturally
- **Implicit cooperation**: Italy-Turkey (never fought despite expansion)
- **Mutual destruction**: Russia-Austria (both ruined each other)
- **Betrayals**: England→Russia (1905-1906), Italy→France (1906)

### Geographic Fate
- **Central powers suffered**: Austria, France, Germany (surrounded)
- **Corner powers thrived**: England, Turkey (one major front)
- **Flexible power succeeded**: Italy (multiple fronts but picked battles wisely)
