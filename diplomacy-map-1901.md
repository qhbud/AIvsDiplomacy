## Special Notes

### Bicoastal Territories
These territories have two coasts and fleets must specify which coast:
- **Spain**: (nc) = north coast, (sc) = south coast
- **St Petersburg**: (nc) = north coast, (sc) = south coast
- **Bulgaria**: (ec) = east coast, (sc) = south coast

### Impassable Borders
- Switzerland (impassable, no units can enter)
- Armies cannot move through water regions
- Fleets cannot move through land-only regions
