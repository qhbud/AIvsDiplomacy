- F Norway HOLD

Norway is also holding. Russia's F Sweden is adjacent, and while we haven't had direct conflict yet, I need to demonstrate that Norway is firmly defended. This hold prevents any opportunistic Russian expansion into my Scandinavian holdings.

The Edinburgh fleet is moving to Norwegian Sea. This repositions naval power northward, creating a strong defensive perimeter around Norway and potentially preparing for future operations against Russia's Sweden or towards St. Petersburg. It also keeps my fleets mobile rather than static.

I'm attempting A Yorkshire to North Sea again. I know this failed last turn because armies can't enter water, but I'm trying to get this army conveyed somehow to the continent. My goal is eventual deployment to Denmark, Holland, or Belgium for land operations.

These moves prioritize defense of my Fall 1901 gains (Denmark, Norway) while building offensive naval positioning. Germany is the immediate threat, Russia is the long-term concern. My alliance with France remains strong - they're expanding south while I hold the north.
