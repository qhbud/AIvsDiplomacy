# Italy - Fall 1910 Pre-Turn Strategic Reflection

**Current Position Analysis:**

I control 9 supply centers, tied with England as co-leader: Marseilles, Rome, Venice, Naples, Vienna, Greece, Tunis, Spain, and Portugal. My Spring 1910 capture of Vienna through the movement cycle was a tactical masterpiece, but I lost Trieste to Austria in the same cycle. My strengths: Mediterranean naval dominance, Vienna gives me Austrian heartland access, strong positioning across multiple theaters. My weaknesses: Austria regained Trieste (my former coastal stronghold), Serbia remains contested for 22+ turns, France's A Tyrolia bounced my Venice attack.

**Fall 1910 Strategic Objectives:**

This is the decisive moment. I'm tied with England at 9 centers, and I must seize every opportunity to break ahead. My analysis reveals several critical targets:

1. **RECAPTURE TRIESTE with supported attack** - Austria has A Trieste after regaining it in the Spring cycle. I can attack with **A Vienna → Trieste** supported by **F Adriatic Sea SUPPORT Vienna → Trieste**. Adriatic IS adjacent to Trieste (I've made this mistake before with Vienna, but Trieste is coastal). This gives me strength 2 vs Austria's likely strength 1 defense. Recapturing Trieste secures my Adriatic position.

2. **CAPTURE BREST from England** - This is a bold opportunistic strike. England has F Brest, but if they move it elsewhere (like to support Holland operations), Brest becomes vulnerable. I have F Mid-Atlantic Ocean positioned perfectly. **F Mid-Atlantic Ocean → Brest** is the move. If England moved F Brest away, I capture their French supply center and deal a psychological blow to my co-leader rival.

3. **Consolidate Austrian elimination** - With Vienna captured and Trieste targeted, I should pressure Budapest as well. **A Piedmont → Tyrolia** then coordinate with Turkey to divide Austria's remaining 3 centers.

4. **Maintain Mediterranean dominance** - Keep fleets positioned defensively: F Portugal HOLD, F Albania supporting Adriatic operations, F Greece contesting Serbia (22nd+ attempt).
